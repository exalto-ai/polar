          What “reported” and “verified” mean
          
          Reported will mean Proof of Thought saw a reviewable AI suggestion arrive through a saved local route. The app name is how you configured that route, while any model name is reported by the tool for that call. Verified will mean a provider-authenticated exchange is bound to the exact accepted change. Neither label says the content is correct.
