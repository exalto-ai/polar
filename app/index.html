      Current access

      This temporary shared setup lets the AI app read, search, create, edit, and move any document in this local Proof of Thought workspace to Trash. Configured AI sessions can use it even when no Proof of Thought window is open, and can edit directly, not only suggest changes.

      When the AI app reads or searches a document, it may send that content to its AI provider under that app’s privacy settings.

      To stop access, remove Proof of Thought from that AI app. Per-reviewer document permissions and in-app revocation are next in this upgrade.

          What “reported” and “verified” mean
          
      Reported means Proof of Thought saw an AI tool edit arrive through a local tool connection. The app and model names are supplied by that client. Verified will mean a provider-authenticated exchange is bound to the exact accepted change. Neither label says the content is correct.
