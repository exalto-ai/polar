          How AI labels work
          
              Reported means Proof of Thought saw AI wording arrive through a local tool connection or built-in chat. Connected app and model names come from that client. Built-in chat records the selected provider and model, but neither path authenticates provider or model identity in the current build. The label never says the content is correct.
